SECTION 11 · ANSWER-FIRST FAQ

Frequently Answered Questions

No marketing hand-waving. Precise technical answers to how the Conserved Ledger behaves on the shop floor.

1. Does TsukiFlow handle multi-stage job-work under Section 143?

Yes. Subcontractor processors are native DAG nodes; Rule 55 challans at dispatch, 9/6/3/1 month aging countdown monitors, gate receipt shrinkage matching, and quarterly ITC-04 JSON exports are built in.

2. Does TsukiFlow replace TallyPrime or SAP?

No replacement required. TsukiFlow acts as the operational sub-ledger. Validated vouchers post into TallyPrime via port 9000 HTTP TDL with UUID deduplication, and to enterprise ERPs via signed webhooks.

3. What happens during shop-floor network outages?

Zero disruption. Touchscreen kiosks run an offline-first state machine with encrypted local SQLite/IndexedDB queues. Offline completions sync idempotently on reconnect; conflicts become human review items, never silent overwrites.

4. What is the deployment timeline for the 30-Day Balance Test?

Live within one week. Masters and BOMs import in 3–5 business days. One routing DAG is drawn with our manufacturing engineer, and kiosks deploy on one line parallel to your existing systems.

5. How are offcuts, turnings, and machining scrap accounted for?

Planned scrap is a routed, valued SKU. It is banked with parent lot lineage. MRP nets it against gross demands, and salvage realization credits the live job's WIP directly in real time.

6. Can YUZU AI post financial vouchers or modify routings automatically?

No. YUZU AI has a strict constitutional limit: it drafts, audits, and surfaces anomalies citing ledger rows, but cannot post vouchers, release disbursements, or alter routings without human engineer sign-off.

7. How do we migrate from spreadsheets or an abandoned ERP?

Through opening GRNs with lots and rates. Masters import cleanly; historical figures arrive as archived reports, never fabricated ledger rows. The pilot runs parallel so nothing cuts over blind.

8. Does TsukiFlow support multi-plant and multi-company operations?

Yes. Config packs per tenant; unlimited warehouses and entities on Flow and above; role-scoped navigation per plant.

9. What GST and statutory outputs does TsukiFlow produce?

HSN and tax type per process and item. Inclusive/exclusive handling; purchase/sales registers and e-invoice-ready payloads derived from posted documents.

10. Why do we claim TsukiFlow is the “first”?

Because conservation is a database constraint. Routing is a disposition graph with rejection paths and co-products, and operations carry their own financial triggers — together by construction.
